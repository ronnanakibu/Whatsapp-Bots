# v5.16.1 — Video Rendering stability & Image Resize safety

## Bug Fixes
- **Vignette Mode Rendering Crash**: Fixed a crash in the playlist and single-song video rendering pipeline when selecting "Vignette Mode" (Dark/Light). Resolved the invalid FFmpeg command option that caused the video generation process to terminate immediately.
- **Custom/AI Artwork Overlay Safety**: Added automatic scaling validation for uploaded or AI-generated cover images. Resizes them to standard 1080p dimensions before applying the song title banner, preventing processing errors caused by dimension mismatches.
