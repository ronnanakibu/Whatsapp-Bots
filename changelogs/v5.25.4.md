# v5.25.4 (Symmetrical 2-Line Meme Layout & Character Midpoint Calibration)

## Features & Improvements
- **Strict 2-Line Regional Cap for Meme Stickers**: Overhauled `.sticker` text formatting to enforce an aesthetic maximum of 2 lines per region (top or bottom), keeping the underlying media uncluttered regardless of input sentence length.
- **Character-Balanced Natural Word Wrapping**: Line breaks are now algorithmically computed against the exact character midpoint of the text, producing clean, evenly distributed, and symmetrical typography while keeping whole words intact.
- **Adaptive Dynamic Typography & Border Scaling**: Text size and contrast strokes now adapt proportionately to line density, preventing clipping and maintaining bold readability across both static and animated stickers.
