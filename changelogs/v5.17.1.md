# Changelog - v5.17.1 (TikTok Playable MP4 Video Format Optimization)

Optimization for TikTok video downloads to guarantee smooth, universal MP4 video playback across all mobile devices and messaging clients.

## User-Facing Features

- **TikTok Universal MP4 Video Compatibility (`!dl / !tiktok`)**
  - **Playable MP4 Standard**: Resolved playback failures on mobile devices by standardizing downloaded TikTok videos into high-compatibility MP4 format (H.264 video codec with AAC audio stream).
  - **Multi-Server Provider Engine**: Enhanced multi-fallback extraction engine for TikTok links, preventing video errors or unplayable format warnings.
  - **Faststart Playback Support**: Videos now stream and play instantly upon downloading without buffering or playback compatibility errors.
