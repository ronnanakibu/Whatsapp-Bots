# v5.25.16 (Resilient Cloud Media Downloader & Smart YouTube Sound Search)

## Features & Improvements
- **Smart YouTube Sound Search Fallback**: When playing audio clips with `.sound <nama/judul>` (e.g., `.sound national anthem ussr` or `.sound sound pki`), if the sound effect is not present in local or meme soundboards, the bot now automatically searches YouTube for the closest matching sound or anthem, downloads the high-fidelity audio track, and plays it instantly as an audio message.
- **Extended Sound Search Duration**: Sound searches (`.sound search <query>`) now index audio tracks, viral anthems, theme songs, and speeches up to 8 minutes in length, allowing quick discovery and playback of rich media sounds.
- **Resilient Cloud Media Extraction**: Media downloading for high-quality audio and video across cloud environments has been significantly stabilized with multi-client streaming adaptors, bypassing player response blocks and ensuring uninterrupted MP3 and MP4 downloads.
- **Local Sound Cache Acceleration**: Online and YouTube-sourced audio clips are automatically cached locally in high-performance storage upon first playback, making subsequent plays 100% instant with zero network latency.
