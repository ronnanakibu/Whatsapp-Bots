# Version 5.17.4 Release Notes

## 🚀 Enhancements & Features

- **Optional Instagram/yt-dlp Cookies Support**:
  - Added support for custom Netscape HTTP cookies via `./storage/cookies.txt` or `IG_COOKIES` environment variable.
  - Allows `yt-dlp` to download age-restricted or private posts directly from Instagram CDN with full account authorization.
