# Changelog v5.14.17

## Feature Enhancements
- **Personal OAuth2 Google Drive Support**: Updated `gdrive.js` to optionally authenticate via the user's personal Google account OAuth2 credentials (using `YOUTUBE_CLIENT_ID`, `YOUTUBE_CLIENT_SECRET`, and `YOUTUBE_REFRESH_TOKEN` from `.env`) instead of the default Service Account. This routes the Google Drive backup uploads directly into the user's personal Google Drive storage, bypassing the new 0-byte quota restrictions on Google Service Accounts. Falls back to Service Account auth if personal OAuth2 variables are not fully configured.
