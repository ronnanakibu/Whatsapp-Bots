# Changelog v5.14.8

## Feature Enhancements
- **Seamless Video Background Looping**: Configured a dynamic post-generation process that creates a seamless "ping-pong" video background loop using FFmpeg. When the short motion video clip (e.g., generated from the static thumbnail image) finishes playing, it plays smoothly in reverse, then normal, preventing any abrupt, disjointed cuts when looped to match the full duration of the music track.
- **Improved Thumbnail Image Color Accuracy**: Re-prioritized the image generation spaces inside the AI service module. Deactivated the `Klein-Multi-LoRA` engine (which was causing a persistent deep purple/violet tint across generated images) and established the clean, photorealistic `black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-schnell` model as the primary generator.
