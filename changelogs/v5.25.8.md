# v5.25.8 (Executive Catch-Up Digest & Intelligent Activity Tracker)

## Features & Improvements
- **Executive Catch-Up ("What Have I Been Missing?")**: Added a personal executive assistant trigger for the bot owner. When returning online and asking in DM (e.g. `what ive been missing`, `what did i miss`, `ketinggalan apa`), the bot automatically analyzes all WhatsApp groups with unread messages since the owner was last active, generates a concise digest per group, and marks them read.
- **Owner Group Activity Tracker**: Integrated continuous background tracking of owner read activity, receipts, and group messages to ensure catch-up digests strictly cover unread dialogue without redundant repeats.
- **Universal Chat Summarizer Command (`.summarize` & `.rangkum`)**: Enabled on-demand summarization for all users and groups via `.summarize [count]` and `.rangkum [count]` supporting 10 to 500 messages with full comprehension of Indonesian conversational slang and community banter.
- **Privacy-First Multi-Group Briefing**: When triggered in group contexts, the executive catch-up digest automatically dispatches to the owner's private chat to safeguard cross-group privacy.
