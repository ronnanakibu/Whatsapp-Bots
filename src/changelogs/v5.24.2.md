# v5.24.2 (Dynamic Online Meme Soundboard Search & Instant Voice Notes)

## Features
- **Online Meme Soundboard Search**: Added full online meme soundboard search support (`.sound <nama>` and `.sound search <nama>`). The bot can now dynamically search, discover, and stream authentic meme sound effects directly from online soundboards in 1-2 seconds.
- **Intelligent Multi-Source Sound Pipeline**: Sound requests now automatically query local custom sounds, built-in classic meme sounds, online meme soundboards, and sound effect libraries for maximum reliability and breadth.
- **Interactive Sound Search Catalog**: Users can now browse search results using `.sound search <query>` (or `.sound cari <query>`) to view top matching sound titles and instantly play any selected sound effect.
- **Automated Sound Caching**: Any sound discovered and played from online searches is automatically cached into persistent storage for instant (0ms) replay on subsequent requests.
