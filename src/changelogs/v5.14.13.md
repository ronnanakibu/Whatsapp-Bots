# Changelog v5.14.13

## Feature Enhancements
- **Dynamic Video & Audio Fade-In/Out**: Reordered the FFmpeg rendering block in `suno.js` to extract the audio duration first. Utilized this duration to dynamically calculate the fade start times and append visual fade-in (`1.5s`), visual fade-out (`2.5s`), audio fade-in (`1.0s`), and audio fade-out (`3.5s`) filters. This makes video and audio tracks transition smoothly from and to black.
