# v5.16.2 — Decoupled Batch Playlist Art Confirmation & Rendering Pipeline

## New Features
- **Dual-Phase Playlist Pipeline**: Re-architected the playlist generation logic into two sequential execution loops:
  - **Phase 1 (Interactive Artwork Generation)**: Resolves titles, visual prompts, audio durations, and generates/confirms cover art for all tracks in a fast, sequential flow.
  - **Phase 2 (Automated Background Processing)**: Generates motion animations, overlays banners, compiles clips via FFmpeg, uploads backup MP3s to Google Drive, and merges the final video in the background.
- **Improved User Experience**: Users no longer have to wait for minutes of video compilation and upload time between individual track artwork confirmations; they can approve all visual covers back-to-back at the beginning.
