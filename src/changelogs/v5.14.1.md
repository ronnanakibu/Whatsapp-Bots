# Changelog v5.14.1

## GDrive Parallelization, Decoder Fixes & Dashboard UX Enhancements
- **Parallel GDrive Uploads**: Shifted the raw MP3 Google Drive upload to trigger in the background immediately after the audio download finishes, running concurrently with video rendering and YouTube uploads.
- **Crypto Decoder Fix**: Refactored the service account authentication layer in `gdrive.js` to manually parse JSON and sanitize private keys (handling escaped `\n` and carriage return `\r` mismatches), preventing decryption decoding failures.
- **Terminal Log Anti-Spam**: Refactored the `updateJob` logs logger to overwrite transient rendering progress percentage metrics in-place rather than printing new entries, keeping logs clean.
- **Smart Scroll Logic**: Replaced window-wide forced scrolling with a localized ref that monitors log container viewport bounds, auto-scrolling only when users are already at the bottom.
