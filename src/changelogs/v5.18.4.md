# Release Notes v5.18.4

## 🚀 Key Improvements & Updates

### ⚖️ Comprehensive AI Judge Synthesis Instruction
- **Detailed Pricing & Specs Breakdown**: Enhanced the Supreme Judge prompt in the AI Swarm engine to strictly require exhaustive, detailed breakdowns when users inquire about prices, products, or specifications.
- **Categorized Product & Model Guidance**: Configured the judge to structure price inquiries into distinct categories (e.g. Standard vs Low Watt vs Inverter), brand-by-brand series price ranges (Daikin, Panasonic, Sharp, LG, Samsung, Gree, Polytron), technical specifications (BTU capacity, Wattage, room m² fit), and practical buyer advice.
- **Structured WhatsApp Output**: Mandated clean list structures and bold header formatting for optimal readability without link/citation clutter.
