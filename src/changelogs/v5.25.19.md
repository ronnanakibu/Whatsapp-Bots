# v5.25.19 (Document & Lecture Material Vault with AI Study Assistant)

## Features & Improvements
- **Document & Material Storage**: Users can now store lecture materials, slides, PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, and personal notes directly in the bot using `.notes add` or `.materi add`.
- **Direct Document Retrieval**: Retrieve stored documents anytime with `.notes get <id>` or `.materi get <id>`, sending original files with intact filenames and metadata back to the chat.
- **AI Study Guide & Exam Prep (UTS/UAS Assistant)**: Summarize complex lecture slides, documents, or notes instantly using `.notes ringkas <id>` or `.materi ringkas <id>`, generating core concepts, key formulas, and exam practice questions.
- **Public & Shared Study Materials**: Mark lecture notes as public or share them across study groups with `.materi add` or `--public` flag so fellow students can access and learn together.
- **Media Downloader Engine Recovery**: Restored binary execution path for the media engine, ensuring uninterrupted video and audio downloading across all cloud and local environments.
