# Release Notes v5.18.5

## 🚀 Key Improvements & Updates

### 🎯 Smart Reply Intent Filter for AI Routing
- **Smart Quoted Reply Filtering**: Implemented an intelligent intent detection mechanism for quoted replies to bot messages (`isSmartReplyFollowup`). Casual banter (such as "wkwk", "haha", "ok", "tq", "siap", "gokil", or emojis) is automatically filtered out to prevent unwanted AI triggers in group chats.
- **Intent Signal Recognition**: Quoted replies are passed to the AI engine if they contain explicit bot mentions, question marks/words (`?`, `gimana`, `kenapa`, `berapa`), follow-up connectors (`jelaskan`, `maksudnya`, `tapi`), or media attachments.
- **Context Window Protection**: Ignored casual banter no longer pollutes the ongoing AI conversation session history or generates unnecessary model calls.
